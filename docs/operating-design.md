# Precision Council for AI: Operating Design

How this site operates. This is the authoritative design the build follows, so the site behaves as one connected precision workbench rather than a set of disconnected tools. It is written to be followed, not admired; where a screen or a prompt is built, it should conform to this.

## What the site is

A workbench a person uses to make an idea, an inquiry, or a document more precise, and then to build confidence in it. It is not a place you bring finished claims to be scored. It is a place that helps you find the claims you are actually making, sharpen them, and see how much they survive. Its one job, in the language of the wider toolkit, is shared measurement of meaning, applied here to the person's own material.

## The core flow: a guided process, not a menu

The site opens as a short guided process (a wizard), not a wall of tools.

1. **Explainer.** A first screen states plainly what the process does and why, in three moves: it helps you generate an idea, refine it, and build confidence in it.
2. **Branch.** It then asks how the person wants to approach it and routes them to the right place, rather than marching everyone through all three phases in order.
3. **Phases.** The three phases below are what the branch routes into.

The seam between steps is the product. A claim surfaced in one step carries into the next without the person re-entering it. Material goes in, candidate claims come out, and those flow into measurement, one continuous path.

## The three phases, each backed by a real instrument

**Generate, by three-plate passes.** Take the raw idea or inquiry and run several independent takes at it, then let what converges across them be the idea that is actually there. The grinding produces the core rather than assuming it. This is the three-plate method used generatively.

**Refine, by PFDS and the standards.** Make that core precise. Operational definitions so a claim is decidable by an independent observer from evidence alone. The sharper Occam's razor to cut what is not load-bearing. The Frame Language check so the words carry their meaning and do not import unearned weight.

**Build confidence, by disconfirmations of various kinds.** Confidence is what survives attack, not what feels right. The kinds include the observer-independence test (do genuinely independent readers reach the same call), an adversarial pass that tries to break the idea, and the Frame Language fake-rigor checks (a claim that wears structural vocabulary without the structure). The council is the instrument here.

## Entry points (the branch)

The branch offers the states a person actually arrives in. Each seeds a phase rather than a blank page.

- **A document.** They have something written and want its claims found and checked. The site surfaces the claims the document contains and offers to send any of them into refine and confidence.
- **An inquiry.** They have an open question they are working out. The site helps form the claims that would answer it, then carries those forward.
- **A framework.** They are working inside a familiar structure (see below). The framework's fields become the named targets the refine phase sharpens.
- **Material.** They have a pile of data or notes and want to generate from it. This is generate, seeded by their material.

## Frameworks as scaffolds, not a catalog

The site does not supply strategy frameworks or theories of change. It makes the person's own framework precise. This distinction is load-bearing: a catalog of frameworks would sprawl into a generic framework-filler and blur the site's one sharp job. Precision applied to a framework the person already uses keeps the engine constant and meets them at a familiar door.

A framework is an informal standard for structuring thinking, and CRAFT is the meta-standard for what makes a standard checkable, so "make your theory of change precise" is CRAFT applied to a theory of change. Frameworks give the refine phase named targets instead of an open field, which makes the process easier, not broader.

First frameworks to support, because both are widely used and notoriously vague so the value shows on contact: Theory of Change, and Rumelt-style strategy. Rumelt's own diagnosis (bad strategy is fluff, restating goals as strategy, refusing to face the actual challenge) is Frame Language pointed at strategy, so his fluff test can be operationalized directly.

## The constraints that keep it honest

- **The tool suggests; the person holds the whole.** The site surfaces candidate claims and questions by maximizing contact with the person's material. It does not decide what the real hypothesis is. The synthesis, holding the whole and choosing the path, stays with the person. A tool handed the framing does the framing badly (it misclassifies an emergent question as an analyzable one), so the site supports sensemaking and does not replace it.
- **Determinations, not scores.** Where the site evaluates, it produces determinations against operational definitions (yes, no, or insufficient evidence, with the evidence quoted), not fuzzy scores. A determination is stable across runs and readers; a score wanders.
- **Repeatability is engineered, not assumed.** Same input, same read comes from three layers: operational determinations, determinism controls (temperature zero, fixed prompt version), and a determination ledger content-addressed on the full input including model version. The ledger is the traceability chain; a model change is an honest recalibration, not silent drift.
- **Observer-independence is the confidence test and the load-bearing claim.** Whether genuinely independent readers reach the same determination from the same evidence and definition is both how confidence is measured here and the claim the whole toolkit rests on. It is treated as an empirical result to be earned, not asserted.

## What exists and what is next

- **Built:** the council (evaluate a claim across independent evaluators) at `/`; the observer-independence test (`/observe`), which runs decidable checks past independent providers and reports whether they agree.
- **Next, in slices:** the wizard front door (explainer and branch); the hypothesis generator (document and inquiry entry points that surface claims from the person's material); framework entry points (Theory of Change, Rumelt) as scaffolds; the determination ledger for repeatability and traceability. The council itself moves from scores toward determinations as this lands.

Everything here serves the flow: the person brings their inquiry, document, or framework, the site helps them find and sharpen the claims in it, and the council reads back how much survives, without ever making them stop and re-enter what they already have.
